## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

### The app won't open

Sometimes macOS blocks apps from unknown developers. Here's how to fix it:

1. Go to **System Settings** (or System Preferences on older Macs)
2. Click **Privacy & Security**
3. Look for a message about AppStoreForge at the bottom
4. Click **Open Anyway**
5. Go back to your Downloads folder and try opening the app again

### The app crashes on launch

Restart your Mac, then try again. Make sure you have at least 200 MB of free disk space.

### My screenshots look blurry

Make sure your original screenshots are high resolution (at least 1200 pixels on the shortest side). Screenshots taken directly from your phone or simulator work best.

### I can't find the export button

At the top right corner of the window, you'll see **Export**. If you don't see it, your window might be too small. Drag the corner of the window to make it bigger.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Better Store Screenshots
